Built from a real education challenge

No child left behind.

Working with Wazir Education Society through Microsoft Give highlighted a clear need: every student’s work should be seen, understood, and answered with guidance that helps the learner move forward.

Every student seen. Every result safeguarded.
Why we created EduGrade

Every student deserves timely guidance—regardless of geography.

Students in rural and tribal communities in Madhya Pradesh can have fewer opportunities for frequent, individualized academic feedback. When work waits too long to be reviewed, a learner may miss the guidance, confidence, and practice needed for the next step.

Built around the education work supported by Wazir Education Society (WES), EduGrade is designed so each learner can receive timely, understandable, bilingual guidance while their academic evidence and personal dignity remain protected.

Student impact Every learner’s work can be seen

Structured review helps reduce the chance that a submission waits too long for attention.

Learning impact Feedback arrives while it can still help

Clear strengths, improvement areas, and next steps support the learner’s next attempt.

Student confidence Progress becomes visible and achievable

Simple feedback helps learners understand what they did well and the one action to take next.

Future opportunity Learning can expand a child’s choices

Consistent participation, practice, and skill-building can prepare students for further study and future work.

End-to-end system architecture

How EduGrade moves from assignment to approved feedback

The architecture separates learner evidence, local media processing, AI assessment, governance, and the educator’s final decision.

Liveable and auditable by design

Evidence moves through six controlled stages

The agent prepares an explainable draft. Source, consent, confidence, and educator controls determine whether feedback can be released.

01
Knowledge grounding

Understand the task

Load the approved class module, assignment instructions, expected evidence, and teacher-defined rubric.

02
Permission check

Validate the submission

Confirm learner registration, consent, assignment match, file type, size, and document integrity.

03
Evidence routing

Collect permitted evidence

Process validated images and PDFs. Raw video stays outside the grading model; only an approved transcript may enter.

04
Draft assessment

Evaluate fairly

Apply each rubric parameter to visible academic evidence and draft bilingual, actionable feedback.

05
Safety and confidence gates

Stop when uncertain

Incomplete sources, wrong assignments, missing evidence, or low confidence pause automated scoring for review.

06
Educator release

Approve the learning outcome

The teacher checks evidence, edits scores and feedback, then releases the final result to the learner.

One governed experience

From trusted knowledge to approved feedback

Each role has a focused responsibility. AI supports the workflow but never owns the final educational decision.

01 · Admin

Ground the agent

Manage curriculum knowledge and class- and subject-aware evaluation rubrics.

02 · Student

Submit securely

Use assigned homework, verified consent, and approved multimodal submission formats.

03 · Teacher

Make the decision

Review evidence and confidence, then approve, edit, discard, or override the AI draft.
